Are utilities and internet included?

Utilities are included in every package. High-speed internet is included in Premium, and Wi-Fi is available throughout the building.

Does sending an enquiry book a room?

No. An enquiry does not confirm a booking or take payment. Our team replies by email to confirm availability and prices.

I have accessibility needs. What do I do?

Choose Custom, or select “Custom package / accessibility needs” on the Contact form. Describe what you need in the message — for example step-free access or an accessible bathroom. Our team replies to discuss what can be arranged and gives you a quote. Sending a message does not commit you to anything.

How long can I stay, and what do I pay in advance?

Short-term stays are 1–4 weeks. Standard and Premium are for longer stays; our team confirms the minimum stay and any advance payment in its reply to your enquiry.

Are there any extra costs?

Utilities are included in every package, and the table above shows what else each one includes. The Enquiry Summary also shows a service fee. Our team confirms the full cost before you decide.

Can I change package later?

Yes — mention it in your enquiry and the team will explain the options for moving between packages.

Our Story

Sky-nest was created for people who move to Singapore for work or study and need a home quickly. Instead of searching for furniture, internet and friends separately, residents move into a furnished room with high-speed Wi-Fi, co-working spaces and weekly community events already in place. Short-term, long-term and custom stays — including corporate bookings and residents with accessibility needs — are arranged directly with our team.
